# Post-Minkowskian (PM) expansion

Expansion in powers of $G$ at **arbitrary velocity** (vs post-Newtonian: small $G$ *and* small $v$). Natural for scattering.

$$\chi(\gamma,j) = \sum_n \frac{2\chi_n(\gamma)}{j^n}$$

- $n$PM term ∝ $G^n$, suppressed at large impact parameter.
- BH: known to 4PM analytically; 5PM fitted to NR → [[Point-mass potential w_BH]]
- Tides start at 6PM → [[Tidal potential w_tid]]

**Problem**: a truncated series in $1/j$ breaks down at small $b$ / large $\chi$. Fix: [[Chi to w map (EOB resummation)]] — in the paper PM-expanded angles are up to ~10× worse than EOB-resummed.
