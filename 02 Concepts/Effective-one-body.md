# Effective-one-body (EOB)

Buonanno & Damour (1999). Maps the two-body problem onto one effective particle moving in a deformed background. Combines PN, PM, self-force, EFT results into a resummed description used for GW templates.

In the scattering context ([[Damour Jain Sperhake 2025 - BS scattering]]):
- Energy variable $\gamma$: $h=E/M=\sqrt{1+2\nu(\gamma-1)}$; equal mass $\gamma = 2(E/M)^2-1$. For scattering, $\gamma$ = relative Lorentz factor.
- Radial potential $w(\gamma,\bar r) = \sum_n w_n(\gamma)/\bar r^n$, $\bar r = \bar R/(GM)$ isotropic EOB radius.
- Mass-shell: radial momentum² $\sim p_\infty^2 + w - j^2/\bar r^2$.
- Decomposition: $w = $ [[Point-mass potential w_BH]] $+$ [[Tidal potential w_tid]] $+$ [[Scalar potential w_scalar]]

The link between $w$ and the [[Scattering angle]] is [[Chi to w map (EOB resummation)]].
