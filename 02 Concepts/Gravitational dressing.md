# Gravitational dressing of the scalar interaction

$O(G)$ corrections to scalar exchange (diagrams 1b–1d: graviton attached to the scalar line). Not computed exactly → **modelled** with a factorised, exponentiated ansatz (Eqs. 14–15):

$$w^{\rm scalar}_{\rm HO} = w^{\rm scalar}_{\rm LO}\cdot\exp\!\left[GM\,\frac{2\omega^2-\mu^2}{\tilde\mu}\,\log\!\left(2e^{\gamma_E}GM\tilde\mu\bar r\right)\right]$$

Where the form comes from: the asymptotic profile of an **isolated** gravitating BS (Eq. 19),
$$A(r)\approx\frac{c_A}{r}\exp\!\left[-\bar\mu_A r + Gm_A\frac{2\omega_A^2-\mu^2}{\bar\mu_A}\log(2e^{\gamma_E}\bar\mu_A r)\right]$$
— gravity adds a log correction to the Yukawa tail. Same structure assumed for the two-body potential.

**Status**: ansatz, not derived. Essential for agreement (LO alone fails). Prime candidate for follow-up work → [[Open questions]].
