# Scattering angle $\chi$

Angle between incoming and outgoing asymptotic directions of a hyperbolic encounter.

- Depends only on $(E, J)$ measured at infinity → **gauge-invariant**.
- PM expansion: $\chi(\gamma,j) = \sum_n 2\chi_n(\gamma)/j^n$, $j=J/(Gm_1m_2)$ → [[Post-Minkowskian expansion]]
- Converted to/from a radial potential via [[Chi to w map (EOB resummation)]].
- Varying impact parameter $b$ at fixed $E$ scans the potential at different radii.

**Extraction from NR** ([[NR scattering setup]]):
1. Trajectory → polar $(r,\theta)$
2. Fit $\theta_p = \sum_{n=0}^p \theta^p_n/r^n$
3. $\bar\theta = (\theta_0^2+\theta_0^3+\theta_0^4+\theta_0^5)/4$
4. Fit error: half range of $\theta_0^p$, $p=1..6$ (≲1.5°); resolution error ~0.5° → total ≲1.6°
5. Average both bodies: $\chi^{\rm NR}=\tfrac12(\chi_1+\chi_2)$
