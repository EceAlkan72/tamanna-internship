# Point-mass potential $w^{\rm BH}$

Black-hole (point-particle) part of the [[Effective-one-body]] radial potential.

- Analytic up to **4PM** (from 4PM $\chi^{\rm BH}$, Damour & Rettegno 2023).
- **5PM** term fitted to NR BH scattering; at this paper's energy: $w_5^{\rm BH} = -1.03\pm0.03$ (interpolated between earlier studies).
- *Effective*: absorbs radiation-reaction effects (overall recoil handled separately).

Alone it describes BS–anti-BS well, since scalar force averages out there → used to calibrate $\epsilon_J$ ([[Initial energy and angular momentum]]).
