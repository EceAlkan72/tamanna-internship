# Tidal potential $w^{\rm tid}$

Finite-size deformation of each star by the other's field.

- Starts at **6PM**: $w_6^{\rm tid}$, $w_7^{\rm tid}$ obtained from known tidal scattering angles via Eq. 7 ([[Chi to w map (EOB resummation)]]).
- Input: even-parity quadrupolar deformability $\Lambda_A = \lambda_A/(G^4m_A^5)\approx 7$ (Sennett et al. 2017).
- Missing: odd-parity quadrupole, even-parity octupole.

**Effect**: negligible at medium/large $b$; at $b=9.9\,GM$ BS–anti-BS improves 3σ → 1σ. Subdominant to [[Scalar potential w_scalar]].
